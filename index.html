2 Add it to your calendar

iPhone / iPad – Add to Calendar Android – Add to Google Calendar

This adds a separate calendar called “EWS work roster”. It doesn’t touch your family or personal calendars, and you can switch it off or delete it in one go. If the roster changes, it updates by itself (usually within a day). Alterations, swaps and leave are not included – always check the alteration sheet.

Help – it didn’t work
  1. iPhone: tap the first button, then Subscribe. Your shifts appear as their own calendar called “EWS work roster”.
  2. Android: the second button opens Google Calendar in your browser. Sign in with the Google account your phone uses and tap Add. It then shows in the Calendar app.
  3. To remove it: iPhone – Calendar app › Calendars › ⓘ next to “EWS work roster” › Unsubscribe. Google – calendar.google.com › Settings › “EWS work roster” › Unsubscribe.
  4. Samsung Calendar or Outlook only: download the calendar file. When it asks where to put the shifts, pick or create a calendar called Work – not your family or personal one – because shifts imported this way can’t be removed in one go.
